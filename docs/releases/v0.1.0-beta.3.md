# ShopAware v0.1.0-beta.3

Users can change their passwords in **My account**. Administrators can create, edit, disable, reset or delete accounts in **Users**, and grant camera access individually or through **Customers** groups. Administrators retain access to all cameras.

Camera permissions now cover API responses, live previews, frames, incident media and reviews. Customer filters are available in the overview, camera list and incident history. Moving a camera clears individual grants and makes its previous incidents admin-only to prevent footage crossing customer boundaries.

The schema 4 upgrade preserves existing credentials and evidence and signs out old sessions. Take a full backup before updating; rollback to beta.2 requires the pre-upgrade database and matching key. See [Users and customers](../USERS_AND_CUSTOMERS.md) and [Server2 deployment](../SERVER2_DEPLOYMENT.md).

YOLO26n detection and YOLO26n pose remain the default models. Detection events still require human review. CPU capacity and detection quality must be qualified with the intended cameras; no GPU is required for this deployment.
