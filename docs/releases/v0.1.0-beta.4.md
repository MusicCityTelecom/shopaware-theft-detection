# ShopAware v0.1.0-beta.4

Beta.4 adds independently selectable per-camera analytics modes while preserving beta.3 users, customer groups, grants and evidence.

## Added

- Shoplifting, Vehicle break-in, LPR and Face Capture mode selection per camera; more than one may be enabled.
- Vehicle break-in review candidates based on explainable temporal person/vehicle interaction signals.
- CPU plate location/OCR vehicle snapshots with the candidate plate outlined, OCR confidence and broad vehicle-color estimates.
- Anonymous face crops grouped only under a continuous per-camera pose track.
- Analytics dashboard with customer filters and server-side camera authorization.
- Parking zones and mode-specific operator/deployment documentation.
- Additive SQLite schema 5 and retention coverage for analytics snapshots.

## Important limits

- Every result requires human verification. A candidate is not proof of theft, break-in, identity or plate ownership.
- Face Capture performs no facial recognition or cross-visit/cross-camera matching.
- Vehicle make/model is not classified in this release.
- The baseline LPR locator/OCR and break-in heuristic require field validation on representative target footage.
- Standard COCO `yolo26n.pt` is required for LPR/vehicle modes; a globally activated specialized Shoplifting model may omit vehicle classes.
- Server2 is CPU-only. Enable new modes one camera at a time and measure capacity.
